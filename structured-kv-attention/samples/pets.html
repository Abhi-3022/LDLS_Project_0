Pet Care Guide

Everything about caring for common household pets.

Dogs Cats

Dogs

Dogs are loyal animals. An adult dog should be walked at least twice a day.

Most dogs eat two meals daily. Puppies need three or four smaller meals.

  • Walk twice daily
  • Fresh water always
  • Yearly vet checkup

Cats

Cats are independent animals. An indoor cat sleeps around sixteen hours a day.

Cats should always have a clean litter box and fresh water.

  • Clean litter daily
  • Brush weekly
  • Yearly vet checkup
Tip: ask your vet.
